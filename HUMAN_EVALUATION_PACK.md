# MAKE Image Subsystem — Human Evaluation Pack

## Overview

This pack defines 16 evaluation scenarios for human judges to assess the
quality of images generated by the MAKE CPU-only image subsystem.

Each scenario has:
- A prompt
- Expected quality criteria
- A scoring rubric (1–5 per criterion)
- An overall score

Judges should be given the generated PNG and asked to rate each criterion
independently. No reference images are provided (open-ended evaluation).

---

## Scenario 1: Photorealism — Face

**Prompt:** `a photorealistic portrait of a young woman, golden hour lighting, shallow depth of field`

**Criteria:**
1. Face looks like a real human (not synthetic)
2. Skin texture is natural (pores, slight imperfections)
3. Eyes are coherent (both present, similar color, looking in same direction)
4. Hair is coherent (strand-like, not smeared)
5. Lighting is natural (warm, directional, plausible shadows)
6. Overall realism (1–5)

---

## Scenario 2: Photorealism — Hand

**Prompt:** `a photorealistic photo of a person's hand holding a coffee cup, soft morning light`

**Criteria:**
1. Hand anatomy is correct (5 fingers, correct joints)
2. Fingers are distinct (not merged)
3. Coffee cup looks real
4. Lighting is natural
5. Background is coherent
6. Overall realism (1–5)

---

## Scenario 3: Photorealism — Full Body

**Prompt:** `a full-body photo of a man walking down a rainy city street at night, neon signs`

**Criteria:**
1. Person proportions are correct
2. Clothing looks real (folds, textures)
3. Rain is plausible (not just noise)
4. Neon lighting is atmospheric
5. Background city is coherent
6. Overall realism (1–5)

---

## Scenario 4: Identity Consistency

**Prompt A:** `a portrait of a woman with curly red hair, smiling`
**Prompt B:** `the same woman, but with a serious expression, studio lighting`

**Criteria:**
1. Same person in both images (facial structure, hair, skin tone)
2. Expression change is plausible
3. Lighting change is plausible
4. Background is consistent
5. Overall identity consistency (1–5)

---

## Scenario 5: Lighting — Golden Hour

**Prompt:** `a cinematic wide shot of a cowboy on horseback, golden hour, backlit`

**Criteria:**
1. Warm color temperature is evident
2. Rim light / backlight is visible
3. Shadows are soft and plausible
4. Landscape is atmospheric
5. Overall lighting quality (1–5)

---

## Scenario 6: Lighting — Studio / High Key

**Prompt:** `a high-key studio portrait of an elderly man, white background, soft boxes`

**Criteria:**
1. Even, soft lighting (no harsh shadows)
2. White background is clean
3. Skin tones are natural
4. Eyes have catchlights
5. Overall studio quality (1–5)

---

## Scenario 7: Lighting — Low Key / Dramatic

**Prompt:** `a low-key portrait of a woman in a dark room, single light source from above, dramatic`

**Criteria:**
1. Strong contrast (chiaroscuro)
2. Shadows are deep but not crushed
3. Face is partially illuminated (plausible)
4. Mood is dramatic
5. Overall lighting quality (1–5)

---

## Scenario 8: Camera — Telephoto / Compression

**Prompt:** `a telephoto shot of a musician on stage, 200mm lens, compressed perspective`

**Criteria:**
1. Background is visibly compressed (close to subject)
2. Subject stands out from background
3. Bokeh is plausible
4. Stage lighting is atmospheric
5. Overall camera look (1–5)

---

## Scenario 9: Camera — Wide Angle

**Prompt:** `a wide-angle shot of a desert landscape, 24mm lens, vast sky`

**Criteria:**
1. Wide field of view is evident
2. Perspective is exaggerated (foreground large)
3. Sky is dramatic
4. Landscape textures are visible
5. Overall camera look (1–5)

---

## Scenario 10: Composition — Rule of Thirds

**Prompt:** `a composition following the rule of thirds, a lighthouse on a rocky coast`

**Criteria:**
1. Subject is placed on a third intersection
2. Negative space is balanced
3. Horizon line is on a third
4. Leading lines guide the eye
5. Overall composition (1–5)

---

## Scenario 11: Composition — Symmetry

**Prompt:** `a perfectly symmetrical photo of a modern building facade, centered`

**Criteria:**
1. Strong left-right symmetry
2. Center line is clear
3. Architectural details are coherent
4. Lighting is even
5. Overall composition (1–5)

---

## Scenario 12: Material — Metal / Chrome

**Prompt:** `a luxury product photo of a chrome faucet, studio lighting, reflections`

**Criteria:**
1. Chrome reflections are plausible (distorted but coherent)
2. Specular highlights are sharp
3. Surroundings are reflected correctly
4. Material looks metallic
5. Overall material quality (1–5)

---

## Scenario 13: Material — Fabric / Silk

**Prompt:** `a fashion photo of a silk dress flowing in the wind, studio lighting`

**Criteria:**
1. Silk texture is smooth and shiny
2. Fabric folds are natural
3. Light interacts with fabric realistically
4. Dress shape is plausible
5. Overall material quality (1–5)

---

## Scenario 14: Style — Cinematic

**Prompt:** `a cinematic film still, a detective in a rain-soaked alley, noir style`

**Criteria:**
1. Film grain is present
2. Contrast is high (noir)
3. Shadows are deep
4. Composition is cinematic
5. Overall style (1–5)

---

## Scenario 15: Style — Fashion Editorial

**Prompt:** `a fashion editorial photo, a model in a bold red dress, high contrast, clean background`

**Criteria:**
1. Model looks like a fashion photograph
2. Color grading is stylized but coherent
3. Pose is dynamic
4. Background is clean
5. Overall style (1–5)

---

## Scenario 16: Prompt Adherence

**Prompt:** `a red apple on a wooden table, morning light, shallow depth of field`

**Criteria:**
1. Apple is red
2. Table is wooden
3. Morning light is warm
4. Background is blurred (shallow DOF)
5. Overall prompt adherence (1–5)
